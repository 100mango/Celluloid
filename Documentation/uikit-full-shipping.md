## Subprocess and deadline review

Raw xcodebuild and simctl commands remain children of the existing run_bounded
owned group. Their phase admission is clipped to the fixed row deadline, and
actual post-return completion must still pass. The existing helper's TERM/KILL
behavior is unchanged; its telemetry is never proof of completed XCTest by itself.
Git/source checks and UIKit screenshot/diagnostic/collection subprocesses do not
create a second process group and remain within that same outer group.

The following helpers already create their own owned native_process groups. This
route therefore invokes them directly, with no newly introduced outer kill:

- Staging requires its full 600-second allowance before dispatch. Its existing
  install ceiling is 300 seconds; each of two container lookups allows 120 seconds
  plus at most 15 seconds of owned cleanup. The inner maximum is 570 seconds,
  leaving 30 seconds for local validation/copying. An explicit 600-second elapsed
  check and row work-deadline check follow; the Actions step is 660 seconds.
  A failed staging step blocks all original test phases, as it did when staging
  lived inside canonical prepare. Unknown lookup cleanup throws before another
  lookup. No install retry is added.
- Original consumer proof requires its full 60-second allowance. Its one summary
  exporter allows 30 seconds plus at most 15 seconds cleanup; local replay has the
  remaining 15 seconds and an explicit 60-second elapsed check. The step cap is
  120 seconds. An exporter exception aborts proof construction.
- Post-test product proof admits the unchanged 60-second container lookup only
  when 15 additional cleanup seconds fit. The existing owned runner is called
  directly. Completion is checked after the command and after local hash reading,
  within the absolute 240-second product phase and 300-second Actions step.
- The Mac producer's summary command has one existing 30-second owned call,
  at most 15 seconds cleanup, and a 120-second step. Its job clock reserves the
  subsequent 300-second source/evidence tail before and after this helper.
- Mac collection anchors the existing optional export deadline to collection
  entry plus 150 seconds. Each exporter reserves its 15-second cleanup inside
  that deadline; the last 30 seconds are for local manifest finalization under
  the unchanged 180-second step. Any unconfirmed optional cleanup disables all
  later exporter calls. An actual elapsed-time check rejects collection overrun.

Bootstrap separately admits every original inner command plus 15 seconds cleanup
against the fixed remaining work deadline. There is no outer kill wrapper, and a
failed or uncertain command aborts the helper before another import/probe. Its
45-minute step can encompass the entire remaining 45-minute work allocation.
No direct helper is permitted to claim success beyond its enclosing step or row
clock. Step/VM termination remains an outer failure boundary, never evidence of
